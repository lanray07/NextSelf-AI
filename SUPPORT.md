- The device you are using

- A short description of the problem
- Screenshots or screen recordings if helpful

## Subscription Help

NextSelf AI subscriptions are managed through Apple. To manage, cancel, or restore a subscription:

1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your Apple ID.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select NextSelf AI.
