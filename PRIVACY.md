# NextSelf AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

NextSelf AI is a wellness and personal growth app. It helps users reflect, journal, track goals, complete missions, and review personal progress.

## Information We Collect

The app may store information you enter into the app, including onboarding details, journal text, voice-journal transcripts, check-ins, goals, habits, missions, progress scores, and app preferences.

If you use voice journaling, the app may request microphone and speech recognition access so it can record and transcribe your reflection. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the app experience, including journals, daily missions, future-self conversations, progress analytics, reminders, and personalization.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers.

## Storage

The app is designed to store user content locally on your device unless a future feature clearly explains otherwise. You can delete app data by deleting the app from your device.

## Children

NextSelf AI is not intended for children under 13.

## Wellness Disclaimer

NextSelf AI is a wellness and personal growth tool only. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, addiction treatment, crisis intervention, or professional healthcare.
